About

The present portfolio gathers some of the works developed during the learning and practice of architecture over the last 5 years.

Within the developed practices, the portfolio focuses on various programs such as cultural, residential, urban planning, and landscaping gained across different experiences from smaller offices to bigger ones, in Portugal's fast-growing construction sector and abroad.

During the practice of architecture, in the professional scope, I developed a wide range of programmatic challenges, which went from the rehabilitation of Portuguese traditional buildings to the design of medium to large-scale services.

Along with the practice and creative process of architecture, I am deeply passionate about graphic design, interiors, and visual representation, motivating me to continuously broaden my knowledge and skills beyond spatial design.
